The fictional municipality of Brookfield publishes monthly tap water measurements in a public dataset. In the 2025 reporting year, technicians collected 240 samples from 20 monitoring points. Each location was sampled once per month. Monitoring points covered treatment outlets, storage facilities, and household distribution areas. The dataset records collection dates, water temperature, turbidity, residual chlorine, and whether the sample was repeated. July had the highest median water temperature, at 23.4 degrees Celsius. January had the lowest, at 6.8 degrees. These observations describe the sampled locations and dates; they do not provide continuous measurements for every household.

The municipality reports monthly medians alongside maximum values so residents can distinguish typical conditions from individual measurements.
