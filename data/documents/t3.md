The fictional municipality of Brookfield releases collection statistics for its 6 public library branches. At the end of 2025, the branches held 312,400 physical items. Printed books accounted for 284,600 items, magazines for 11,800, and audiovisual materials for 16,000. The central branch held 118,200 items. The branch collection table is cited at http://127.0.0.1:8731/source/library-collection-table. Counts describe holdings on the final reporting date rather than every item owned during the year.

Libraries added 18,600 physical items and withdrew 12,300 during the year. Withdrawals included damaged copies, superseded editions, and materials removed after collection reviews. Children's books represented 29 percent of printed books across all branches. Branch percentages ranged from 21 to 38 percent. These differences reflect collection composition and do not measure reading ability or demand in the surrounding neighbourhoods.

The catalogue methods note, cited at http://127.0.0.1:8731/source/library-catalogue-methods, excludes licensed electronic titles from physical holdings. Electronic access covered 24,500 titles under separate agreements. Multiple copies of a printed title are counted as separate items, while an electronic title is counted once within its licence category. A catalogue audit found 460 records awaiting location updates. Those items remained in the municipal total but were reported separately from confirmed branch holdings.
