The fictional municipality of Brookfield reports bus usage through an annual public transport dataset. During 2025, its 18 routes recorded 4,860,000 passenger boardings. Counts represent boarding events rather than individual people, so a passenger changing buses can appear twice. Route 4 carried 720,000 boardings, the largest annual total. Route 17 carried 84,000, the smallest. The route totals table is cited at http://127.0.0.1:8731/source/bus-route-totals.

Weekdays accounted for 76 percent of annual boardings. The busiest recorded month was October, with 462,000 boardings, while February recorded 331,000. School holidays and the number of operating days differed between months. The table therefore includes operating days alongside total boardings. It does not estimate the effect of weather or identify why particular passengers travelled.

The service register, cited at http://127.0.0.1:8731/source/bus-service-register, lists 268,000 scheduled departures and 261,400 completed departures. Three routes had temporary diversions during bridge maintenance. Their counts remain assigned to the original route identifiers. Automatic counters supplied 94 percent of boarding records, and manual surveys supplied the remainder. The published notes explain that fare revenue and passenger boardings are separate measures. Free transfers and concessionary fares mean that revenue cannot be inferred directly from the boarding totals.
