The fictional municipality of Brookfield publishes an inventory of street trees surveyed during 2025. Survey crews recorded 9,600 trees along 142 kilometres of public roads. Plane trees accounted for 2,880 records, ginkgo trees for 2,400, and maples for 1,920. Other identified species accounted for 2,160 records, while 240 trees lacked a confirmed species. The inventory table is cited at http://127.0.0.1:8731/source/street-tree-inventory. Trees in private gardens and municipal woodland are outside its coverage.

Crews measured trunk diameter at a standard height and assigned each tree a location identifier. The median diameter was 24 centimetres. Trees below 10 centimetres represented 14 percent of measured records. Inspectors marked 384 trees for follow-up assessment because of visible damage or restricted growing space. This flag indicates a need for another inspection and does not establish that removal is necessary.

The survey protocol, cited at http://127.0.0.1:8731/source/street-tree-survey-protocol, describes visual species identification and location checks. Diameter measurements were missing for 96 trees where access was obstructed. These records remain in species totals but are excluded from diameter summaries. The municipality plans to revisit unconfirmed species in the following survey cycle. Published totals describe the inventory date and do not account for subsequent planting, storm damage, or removal.
